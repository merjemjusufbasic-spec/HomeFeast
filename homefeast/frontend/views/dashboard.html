Chicken Alfredo Primavera
Ingredients
  • 300 g fettuccine
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 200 ml cream
  • 50 g parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup broccoli
Instructions
  1. Cook the pasta in salted water.
  2. Sear the chicken and slice it.
  3. Simmer cream with garlic and parmesan.
  4. Toss everything together and serve.
